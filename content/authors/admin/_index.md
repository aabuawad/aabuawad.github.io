Ahlam is currently a postdoctoral fellow at the [Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth](https://geiselmed.dartmouth.edu/) researching early life arsenic exposures, metabolomics, and gestational diabetes. 

As a doctoral graduate from the [Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health](https://www.publichealth.columbia.edu/) [Department of Environmental Health Sciences](https://www.publichealth.columbia.edu/academics/departments/environmental-health-sciences), all of her primary research focus was on arsenic. Throughout her doctoral training, Ahlam become an expert on arsenic exposure, toxicity, adverse health-related outcomes and methods of intervention and prevention. Understanding the pathophysiology of arsenic is important for cancer prevention because arsenic has been associated with cancers of the bladder, lung, liver, and skin.
